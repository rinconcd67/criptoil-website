Projects and procurement

Tanks, pipe, valves, telemetry and refinery supply to keep production and processing running, delivered through qualified mills, manufacturer authorized providers and certified contractors.

Illustrative image of distillation columns in heavy lift transport; not a CRIPTOIL operation. Photo: Carlos Valenzuela, CC BY-SA 4.0, source, cropped.

What we supply

Six product lines for upstream, midstream and refining operations. Standards are cited as reference; the purchase order defines each supply.

Storage tanks

  • Welded storage tanks to API 650 and low pressure tanks to API 620.
  • Inspection, repair and alteration materials to API 653.
  • Floating roofs, seals, heating coils and tank accessories.

Illustrative image, not a CRIPTOIL facility. Photo: Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress, public domain.

Pipe

  • Seamless casing and tubing to API 5CT, grades such as L80 and P110, and slotted liners for sand control in heavy oil wells.
  • Line pipe to API 5L for crude, product and gas pipelines.
  • Process piping to ASTM A106 and A53, flanges to ASME B16.5 and fittings to ASME B16.9.

Photo: our team's supply archive, Houston.

Valves

  • Gate, globe and check valves to API 600, API 602 and API 594.
  • Ball and pipeline valves to API 608 and API 6D, fire tested to API 607.
  • Wellhead and tree equipment to API 6A, and valve actuators.

Photo: our team's supply archive, Houston.

Telemetry and instrumentation

Telemetry and instrumentation

  • Pressure, differential pressure, temperature and flow transmitters.
  • Radar tank gauging and inventory monitoring.
  • RTU, PLC and SCADA systems and wireless telemetry for wells, tanks and pipelines.
  • Equipment certified for hazardous areas (ATEX, IECEx, Class I Division 1).

Refining

  • Process equipment, spare parts and turnaround materials for refineries.
  • Centrifugal pumps to API 610 and shell and tube heat exchangers to API 660 and TEMA.
  • Column internals, refractories, gaskets and fasteners.

Illustrative image, not a CRIPTOIL facility. Photo: Antony-22, CC BY-SA 4.0, source, cropped.

Power generation

Power generation

  • Gas fired generation with aeroderivative and industrial gas turbines and reciprocating engines from leading manufacturers.
  • Substations, switchgear and 50/60 Hz frequency conversion, through qualified contractors.
  • Operation and maintenance through manufacturer authorized providers.

Supply of equipment is subject to export controls and sanctions review and requires the authorizations applicable to the destination.

Quality and traceability

Each shipment carries the documentation specified in the purchase order, such as material certificates to EN 10204 3.1 or 3.2, inspection reports and packing lists.

Third party inspection and hold points are agreed with the client before manufacturing starts.
