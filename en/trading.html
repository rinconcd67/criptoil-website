Execution rules

FOB

Delivery at the loading terminal onto a vessel nominated by the buyer and accepted after vessel screening. Quantity and quality are certified at loading by the independent inspector appointed under the contract.

CIF and CFR

Freight, and insurance under CIF, are arranged to the agreed discharge port. Quantity and quality are verified at loading and at discharge.

Independent inspection

Quantity and quality are verified by an internationally recognized independent inspector appointed under the contract. Incoterms 2020 apply.

Who we work with

We contract with the owner of the goods or with a party holding a written mandate to buy or sell on its behalf. We identify the full transaction chain during KYC and decline transactions whose chain cannot be verified. We do not ask for advance fees, deposits, inspection fees or commissions before a written agreement signed by both parties is in place.
